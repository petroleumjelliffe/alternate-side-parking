# Alternate Side Parking: The Game
Game is in early stages of development

## Relive the frustration of trying to park during morning rush hour in New York City!
- Look for an open spot on a crowded street, and park your car before your patience runs out!
- Don't be fooled by Fire Hydrants and Driveways!
- State of the art automatic transmission lets you easily switch between Drive and Reverse.
